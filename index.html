01 // CORE COMPETENCIES & METHODOLOGY

Modern Alpine Coaching Architecture

There is no single rigid dogma in ski racing—only physics, biomechanics, and what makes an athlete faster down the fall line. I test every concept on my own skis before teaching it.

SYS.01 // BIOMECHANICS & LINE

Trajectory & Force Physics

Integrating motorsport racing-line theory with European technical mechanics: optimizing apex timing, shin-to-boot leverage, hip projection, and pressure management across gate transitions.

SYS.02 // ACTIVE DEMONSTRATION

Live On-Snow Execution

Unlike retired legacy coaches, I still ski sub-35 FIS point speeds. I experience the exact snow-surface chatter, ruts, and injection conditions as my athletes and demonstrate corrections in real time.

SYS.03 // INDIVIDUAL CALIBRATION

Anthropometric & Mental Tuning

No two racers move alike. I calibrate technical cues, tactical risk profiles, and off-season dryland bridging to each athlete's height, leverage ratios, strengths, and psychology.

SYS.04 // MODERN KNOWLEDGE TRANSFER

High-Signal Video & Digital Lab

Translating complex race telemetry and high-frame-rate video analysis into clear, direct, ego-free adjustments on the hill and through @kingjulianhq educational breakdowns.
