02 // METHODOLOGY & SKILLS

Modern Alpine Coaching Architecture

No rigid dogma—only physics, biomechanics, and what makes an athlete faster down the fall line. Every principle is tested on my own skis first.

SYS.01 // BIOMECHANICS & LINE

Trajectory & Force Physics

Blending motorsport racing-line theory with European technical mechanics: apex timing, shin-to-boot leverage, hip projection, and transition pressure.

SYS.02 // ACTIVE DEMONSTRATION

Live Sub-35 FIS Point Execution

I still ski active FIS speeds—feeling the exact snow chatter, ruts, and injection conditions as my athletes to demonstrate real-time corrections.

SYS.03 // INDIVIDUAL CALIBRATION

Anthropometric & Mental Tuning

Calibrating technical cues, tactical risk profiles, and dryland preparation to each racer's leverage ratios, anatomy, and competitive mindset.

SYS.04 // DIGITAL LAB

High-Frame-Rate Video Breakdown

Translating race telemetry and video analysis into direct, ego-free adjustments on the hill and via @kingjulianhq educational content.
